The Recovery Tool: Minimal Input, Powerful Results

The Recovery Tool: Minimal Input, Powerful Results

Published: December 31, 2025

When a cryptocurrency position is underwater, investors often wonder how much more they need to invest to bring their break-even price down to a desired level. The recovery tool answers this question with remarkable precision while requiring only four simple inputs.

You provide your current total quantity held, your current break-even price, the present market price, and your target recovery percentage. From these minimal values, the tool calculates exactly how much additional capital—at the current market price—would achieve your chosen recovery goal.

Why Minimal Input Matters

Many recovery calculators overwhelm users with complex forms and assumptions. This tool takes the opposite approach: it asks only for what is absolutely necessary and handles all complexity behind the scenes. This design makes it accessible to beginners while still delivering sophisticated results trusted by experienced traders.

The target recovery percentage is particularly intuitive. Setting it to fifty percent means you want to close half the gap between your current break-even and the market price. Twenty-five percent targets a quarter of the gap, while higher values aim for more aggressive recovery.

How the Calculation Works

Once inputs are provided, the tool determines the mathematical relationship between additional investment and resulting break-even price. It then uses an efficient iterative search to find the exact additional amount that produces your target break-even. This process happens instantly and invisibly, presenting only the clear final recommendation.

Key Features

  • Instant results with no page reloads
  • Precise additional quantity and dollar amount needed
  • Clear explanation of the resulting new break-even price
  • Graceful handling of impossible scenarios

Real-World Application

Imagine holding one bitcoin with a break-even of sixty thousand dollars while the market sits at forty thousand. Setting recovery to fifty percent tells the tool you want a new break-even of fifty thousand dollars. It immediately responds with the exact additional investment required—typically around forty thousand dollars—to achieve that midway point through averaging down.

Lower percentages require smaller additional buys for partial recovery, while higher percentages demand larger commitments. The tool handles all cases accurately, including informing you when full recovery to current market price is mathematically impossible with finite investment.

By hiding complexity behind a simple interface, the recovery tool empowers investors to make informed decisions about position management during drawdowns without needing advanced mathematical knowledge.
