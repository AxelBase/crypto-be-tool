Handling Edge Cases: Already Profitable or Full Recovery

Handling Edge Cases: Already Profitable or Full Recovery

Published: December 31, 2025

While most uses of the recovery tool involve underwater positions, real-world scenarios include cases where no recovery is needed or where the requested recovery is mathematically impossible. The tool handles both situations thoughtfully to provide clear, honest guidance.

When your current break-even price is already at or below the market price, you are in profit. In these cases, the tool immediately recognizes this and informs you that no additional investment is required to improve your position.

When You Are Already Profitable

Entering a market price higher than your break-even triggers a positive message confirming your favorable status. This prevents unnecessary suggestions and reassures users during bull markets that their averaging strategy has succeeded.

The message is direct and encouraging, allowing you to celebrate gains rather than mistakenly thinking action is needed. This edge case handling ensures the tool remains useful across all market conditions.

The Limits of Full Recovery

A common desire during deep drawdowns is to bring the break-even price all the way down to the current market price through additional buying. Many investors are surprised to learn this goal is fundamentally impossible with any finite investment.

Setting recovery to one hundred percent triggers a special explanation: the target break-even would need to equal or fall below the purchase price of new coins, which averaging cannot achieve. No matter how much you invest, the new break-even approaches but never reaches the market price asymptotically.

Why This Matters

  • Prevents unrealistic expectations during severe dips
  • Educates users about the mathematics of averaging down
  • Encourages setting achievable partial recovery targets
  • Avoids misleading or infinitely large recommendations

Practical Guidance

Instead of aiming for impossible full recovery, users can select ninety or ninety-five percent to see substantial but realistic additional investments. These high percentages still dramatically lower break-even while remaining mathematically sound.

By addressing these edge cases directly rather than forcing calculations, the tool builds trust through transparency. It respects mathematical reality while guiding users toward practical, achievable recovery strategies in any market environment.

Understanding these boundaries is key to using the recovery tool effectively and maintaining realistic expectations during portfolio management.
