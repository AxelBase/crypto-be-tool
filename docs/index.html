About the Crypto Break-Even Portfolio Utility

The Crypto Break-Even Portfolio Utility is a free, open-source, fully client-side web application designed to empower cryptocurrency investors with clear, accurate insights into their portfolio positions. Built with privacy and simplicity in mind, it eliminates the guesswork surrounding break-even prices and recovery planning in volatile markets.

Unlike many online calculators that require accounts or send data to servers, this utility processes everything directly in your browser. No information is transmitted, stored, or logged, ensuring complete data isolation and security. Whether you are managing a single asset or tracking multiple purchases across time, the tool provides precise weighted average calculations that include transaction fees—an often-overlooked factor that significantly impacts real cost basis.

The core philosophy centers on break-even psychology: understanding your true average entry price helps you make rational decisions instead of emotional ones during market swings. The Portfolio Calculator allows unlimited dynamic entries with real-time updates, while the Recovery Tool uses advanced iterative optimization to recommend exact additional investments needed to achieve your desired recovery target.

From beginners practicing dollar-cost averaging to experienced traders planning position recovery during drawdowns, this utility delivers professional-grade mathematics in an accessible interface. It handles edge cases intelligently, such as positions already in profit or mathematically impossible full recoveries, and educates users through transparent results.

Every calculation—from simple weighted averages to binary search convergence for recovery scenarios—is performed locally using pure JavaScript, ensuring instant feedback without server dependency. This stateless design makes it ideal for GitHub Pages deployment and guarantees that refreshing the page resets everything, preserving privacy by default.

Ultimately, the utility aims to foster disciplined investing by giving you the numbers you need to set realistic targets, evaluate averaging strategies, and navigate cryptocurrency markets with confidence. It is a community-driven tool focused on accuracy, transparency, and user control.

How to Use the Utility

Three simple paths to clearer portfolio insights—everything happens instantly in your browser.

1

Calculate Your Current Break-Even

Navigate to the Portfolio page. Add as many purchase entries as needed by clicking "Add Entry." For each row, input the quantity acquired, price per coin, and any transaction fee. The break-even price updates in real time as you type.

Fees are fully included in the weighted average calculation (total cost divided by total quantity), providing an accurate reflection of your actual investment. Remove entries easily if needed. This dynamic interface supports dozens of transactions without performance issues.

2

Plan Recovery Strategies

Go to the Recovery Tool page. Enter only four values: current total quantity, current break-even price (from the Portfolio tool or known), current market price, and your target recovery percentage (0–100).

The tool instantly runs background simulations using binary search optimization to determine the precise additional investment required at the current market price to achieve your target new break-even. Results include the recommended buy amount, new break-even price, and clear strategy text.

3

Learn and Refine

Explore the Blog section for in-depth explanations of break-even mathematics, recovery limits, floating-point precision, and edge cases. Use the insights to experiment with different recovery percentages or purchase scenarios.

Since the site is stateless, feel free to test multiple what-if situations safely. Combine outputs from both tools to inform real decisions—always verify independently and consider broader market factors.
