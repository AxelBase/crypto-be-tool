Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: December 31, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to the Crypto Break-Even Portfolio Utility ("the Utility"), a free, open-source, client-side web application designed to help cryptocurrency investors calculate break-even prices and recovery strategies. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices. We prioritize your privacy by operating on a zero-data-collection principle. The Utility runs entirely in your browser, and we do not collect, store, transmit, or process any personal information.

2. No Data Collection

The Utility is built as a static site with all calculations performed locally on your device using JavaScript. We do not collect:

  • Personal information such as names, email addresses, or wallet addresses.
  • Usage data including IP addresses, browser details, device information, or geolocation.
  • Portfolio data – any quantities, prices, fees, or recovery inputs you enter remain exclusively on your device.
  • Analytics or tracking data – no cookies, pixels, fingerprints, or third-party analytics tools are used.

Because everything happens client-side, your inputs never leave your browser. We have no servers that receive or log data.

3. How the Utility Works

All features – portfolio entry management, weighted break-even calculations, and recovery simulations – execute purely in your browser. Inputs are processed in real time using native JavaScript functions. Results are displayed instantly without any network requests to external services. Refreshing the page resets all fields, ensuring complete data isolation.

4. Third-Party Services

We do not integrate advertising, analytics, or tracking services. The only external resources are Bootstrap CSS and JS loaded via public CDN for styling and layout. These resources do not collect personal data or track user behavior in this context.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

The Utility is completely stateless. We do not use cookies, localStorage, sessionStorage, or any persistent storage mechanisms. No information persists between sessions.

6. Data Security

Since no data is transmitted or stored by us, the primary security responsibility lies with your device and browser. We recommend using an up-to-date browser and secure connection (HTTPS) when accessing the Utility.

7. Children's Privacy

The Utility is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

9. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our public GitHub repository.

Your privacy is protected by design. By using the Crypto Break-Even Portfolio Utility, you acknowledge this no-collection policy and enjoy full control over your data.
